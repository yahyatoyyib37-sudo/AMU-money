Your launch progress

30-day launch path

A calm, sequential plan that takes you from zero skill to your first client outreach over one month. Outcomes depend on your skills, effort and market demand.

Week 1 — Skill foundation & Shariah check

Days 1–7
1 · Pick one niche

Choose a single stream based on your hardware and available hours. Do not split focus.

2 · Set up your toolkit

Open free accounts on ChatGPT, Canva, Notion and the AI tools your niche needs.

3 · Build 3 samples

Create three mock case studies so you have proof of capability before you pitch.

Week 2 — Portfolio & presence

Days 8–14
1 · Free portfolio

Publish work on Google Drive, Behance, Notion or GitHub Pages — clean and fast.

2 · Profile polish

Set up Fiverr and LinkedIn around speed, clarity and revisions rather than price.

3 · Price to start

Offer entry pricing to your first three clients to build reviews and trust quickly.

Week 3 & 4 — Ethical outreach & first earnings

Days 15–30
1 · Ten messages a day

Reach local businesses and creators with tailored, genuinely useful suggestions.

2 · Lead with free value

Send a quick audit, sample edit or rewrite snippet that proves the skill upfront.

3 · Deliver & reinvest

Ship on time, over-deliver once, then reinvest earnings into better tools.
